**Section VI-E / Supplementary H.10 — closed-loop evaluation of the candidate memory profile (replaces "the guard remains experimental because fixed motion omits the feedback of changed actions").**

To evaluate the candidate profile in closed loop without retraining, the three frozen S policies were re-evaluated on the nine development conditions with the evaluation camera streams (base seed 1000) under the archived memory rules (`paper_v25`) and under the combined candidate (`guard_v26`: displacement-consistent propagation plus the inward 15 m crossing guard). The `paper_v25` arm reproduces the archived records exactly (27/27 settled, no violation, 9/27 override episodes). The candidate arm also completes 27/27 without a signal or curve-speed violation and has the same number of override episodes, 9/27, but at different locations: eight of the nine archived override episodes are override-free, while eight new override episodes appear, seven of them signal-terminal stops in the cold-pack conditions 3/4/6 and one a near-line colour error. Fallback substeps fall from 96 to 35 and mean travel time by 3.7 s, whereas mean $I_j$ rises from 88.1 to 98.8 m$^2$/s$^5$ (11 of 27 pairs lower, 16 higher) and peak jerk from 10.0 to 12.2 m/s$^3$. The guard rejected seven range updates, all between 2977 and 2991 m; two were followed by an override within four substeps. Because the policies were trained under the archived propagation, the change also alters every trajectory where the guard never fires. The candidate therefore changes where emergency actions occur rather than reducing them, and it is retained as an experimental profile, not adopted as an improved executor.

**Section VI-C — learning increment (replaces the sentence on the historical probe).** Under the current protocol with the same detector, memory and executor, the constant-command probe completes 9/9 and the matched S0 policy differs from it by a mean of $+0.14$ in R (median $+0.00$), $-14.0$ in $I_j$ (median $-0.03$) and $+0.5$ s in time over the nine paired conditions; the differences concentrate in conditions 0, 4 and 7, two conditions are executor-dominated and identical, and in three conditions the constant command scores higher. **Section VI-B — extension conditions of the timing-use intervention (new paragraph).** The frozen actor A was evaluated on the eighteen prespecified reporting conditions of the same route (three packs, initial speeds 14 and 18 m/s, offsets 15/45/75 s), which had never been generated or used for selection. Both executors complete 18/18 without violations or fallback. The timing executor received the countdown in 2849 substeps and performed 909 green-end tests but never changed the selected action, so all eighteen paired trajectories are identical and every window and full-trip cost difference is zero.
